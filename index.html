Kitchen rules that save food

First in, first out. Prioritize fresh vegetables, milk, bread, bananas, and opened cans. Freeze bread and ripe peeled bananas before they spoil.
Cool and store safely. Refrigerate perishables within two hours. Cool rice quickly (ideally within one hour); refrigerate leftovers in shallow containers and use within 3–4 days or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165°F.
Prep across days. A base made for meals more than 3–4 days away should be frozen promptly, not held in the fridge all week.
Budget note. A “can” means an approximate can-equivalent quantity. If you open one, move unused food to a covered container, refrigerate promptly, and use soon or freeze.
